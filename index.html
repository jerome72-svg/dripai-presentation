IMPORTANT PROJECT DETAILS

Clear expectations from day one.

Scope & revisions

There is no arbitrary page limit within the agreed project scope. Reasonable revisions are included during development so the agreed deliverables meet the requirements. A full custom portal, unrelated software, major redesigns or substantial new functionality are outside the agreed scope and will be quoted separately for approval.

Vehicle information

Vehicle listings use dealership-supplied photos and details, plus information published on your website. Live external inventory synchronisation is not included. The AI assistant is configured not to invent vehicle prices, specifications or availability. When it cannot answer reliably, it can collect the visitor's details for your team.

Ownership & platform

Your dealership retains ownership of its business assets, domain, branding and supplied content. DripAI provides the managed platform service while the subscription is active. If service is cancelled, hosted services may end; reasonable notice and handover or migration arrangements will be discussed.

Support & response

Support is provided by email, with a target response time of one business day. This is not a 24/7 human support service.

AI accuracy

The assistant uses the approved information available to it and is designed to hand uncertain enquiries to your team. It does not guarantee sales, conversions or revenue outcomes.

Third-party services

Some services may depend on third-party platforms. Any separately chargeable service or additional cost will be disclosed and approved before it is incurred.
